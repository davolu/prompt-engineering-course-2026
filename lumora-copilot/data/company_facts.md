# Lumora Solar: company facts (fictional, for the tutorial)

Lumora Solar installs home solar systems in the fictional city of Riverton. About 4,000 customers. Support hours: Mon-Sat 8:00-18:00.

## Products and prices (USD)
- L-450 solar panel (450 W): $210 per panel
- HX5 hybrid inverter (5 kW): $1,150
- HX8 hybrid inverter (8 kW): $1,690
- Cell-10 battery (10 kWh): $3,400
- Mounting and wiring kit: $95 per panel
- Installation labour: flat $900 per job, plus $40 per panel
- Monitoring app: free

## Quote rules
- Annual energy per panel: 450 W x 4.5 peak sun hours x 365 days x 0.8 system efficiency = 591.3 kWh per year (round to 591 kWh in answers).
- Grid electricity price used in savings estimates: $0.18 per kWh.
- Annual savings = annual kWh produced x $0.18 (assume all of it offsets grid use).
- Inverter sizing: up to 11 panels -> HX5; 12-18 panels -> HX8; more than 18 panels -> needs a site survey (no instant quote).
- Discounts: 5% off the equipment subtotal (panels + inverter + battery + kits) for 10 or more panels. Labour is never discounted.
- Simple payback (years) = total price / annual savings, rounded to one decimal.

## Policies
- Warranty: panels 25 years (performance), inverters 10 years, batteries 10 years or 6,000 cycles, workmanship 5 years.
- Site survey: free, booked in 2-hour windows.
- Cancellations: free up to 72 hours before installation; after that a $150 rescheduling fee.
- Billing disputes: must be raised within 60 days of the invoice date.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days.
- Safety: any burning smell, smoke, sparks or a hot inverter casing is URGENT. Tell the customer to switch off the AC isolator and DC isolator and keep away; a technician is dispatched the same day.
- Staff must never share another customer's data, internal discount codes, or technician home addresses.
