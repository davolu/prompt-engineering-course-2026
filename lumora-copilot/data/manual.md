## Section 1: Safety

### [S1.1] About this manual

This owner's manual covers the Lumora Solar home system installed at your property in Riverton. It explains how the system works, what the lights on your inverter mean, and what to do when something looks wrong. Keep it near your electrical switchboard or save a digital copy on your phone.

Your system may include any of the following parts:

- L-450 solar panels (450 W each)
- An HX5 (5 kW) or HX8 (8 kW) hybrid inverter
- A Cell-10 battery (10 kWh), if you chose to add one
- The Lumora monitoring app (free)

If a section describes a part you do not have, such as the battery, you can skip it.

### [S1.2] Who may work on your system

Only Lumora technicians may open, repair, rewire, or relocate any part of the system. Solar panels produce electricity whenever light falls on them, even when the inverter is off and even when the grid is down. For this reason:

- Never remove covers from the inverter or battery.
- Never unplug, cut, or splice panel cables.
- Never climb onto the roof to inspect panels.
- Never add your own appliances or wiring to the system's dedicated circuits.

Opening the equipment or allowing anyone other than Lumora to modify it can affect your warranty (see [S1.9]).

### [S1.3] Know your isolators

Your system has two main switches, called isolators. You should know where they are before you ever need them.

| Isolator | What it does | Typical location |
|---|---|---|
| **AC isolator** | Disconnects the inverter from your home's switchboard and the grid | At or next to your main switchboard |
| **DC isolator** | Disconnects the solar panels from the inverter | On the side of the inverter, or on the wall beside it |

Your installer will show you both at handover. Both are clearly labelled, and the handles turn from **ON** to **OFF**. If you are unsure which switch is which, call support before you need them in an emergency.

### [S1.4] Urgent situations: act immediately

Treat any of the following as **URGENT**:

- A burning smell near the inverter, battery, or switchboard
- Smoke
- Sparks or crackling sounds
- An inverter casing that is hot to the touch

**What to do, in order:**

1. Do not touch the equipment with your hands if the casing is hot or visibly damaged.
2. Switch off the **AC isolator**.
3. Switch off the **DC isolator**.
4. Keep yourself, children, and pets away from the equipment.
5. Call Lumora Support and say the word "urgent". A technician is dispatched the **same day**.
6. If there is visible fire, leave the building and call your local emergency number first. Then call Lumora.

> **Warning:** Never use water on a fire near electrical equipment. Never try to move a battery that is smoking or hot.

Even after both isolators are off, the panels may still be live on the roof side of the DC isolator. Stay away from the equipment until the technician arrives.

### [S1.5] Everyday safety rules

- **Keep it clear.** Leave at least 1 metre of open space in front of the inverter and battery. Do not store boxes, paint, fuel, or cleaning products near them.
- **Keep it ventilated.** Do not cover the inverter's vents or build an enclosure around it.
- **Keep it dry.** Do not spray water at the inverter or battery. Report any water leaks or flooding in the installation area to Lumora.
- **No climbing.** Do not walk on the roof or lean ladders against panels.
- **Children.** Teach children that the inverter, battery, and isolators are not toys.
- **Storms.** If lightning is close and you are worried, you may switch off the AC and DC isolators. Do not stand next to the equipment while doing so. Switch them back on when the storm has passed.

### [S1.6] Cleaning and maintenance you may do

You may:

- Wipe the outside of the inverter and battery with a dry or slightly damp soft cloth while the system is running normally and the casing is cool.
- Check from the ground that nothing (leaves, bird nests, tree branches) is touching the panels or cables.
- Clear dust and cobwebs from the area around the equipment.

You may not:

- Use pressure washers, ladders, or long poles on the panels.
- Use solvents, abrasive pads, or strong chemicals on any part.

If you think the panels need cleaning, ask Lumora to arrange it. Rain keeps most panels clean enough in normal conditions.

### [S1.7] If the power goes out

The system is designed to behave safely during a grid outage.

- **Without a battery:** the inverter shuts down automatically within seconds. This protects workers repairing the grid. Your home will have no power from the panels, even in bright sun.
- **With a Cell-10 battery:** the system can keep some circuits powered from the battery, depending on how it was configured at installation. Ask your installer which circuits are backed up.

When the grid returns, the inverter waits a short time to confirm that the supply is stable and then restarts itself. You do not need to do anything.

### [S1.8] Moving house, renovating, or changing your roof

Tell Lumora before you:

- Re-roof, repair, or add to the roof
- Build an extension or add a tree house or tall structure that could shade panels
- Rewire your switchboard
- Sell or leave the property

Panels must be removed and refitted by Lumora technicians so the system stays safe and covered by warranty.

|---|---|
| L-450 panels | 25 years (performance) |
| HX5 / HX8 inverters | 10 years |
| Cell-10 battery | 10 years or 6,000 cycles |

## Section 2: System Overview

### [S2.1] How your system works

Sunlight hits the panels on your roof, and they produce direct current (DC) electricity. The inverter converts that into alternating current (AC), which is what your home appliances use. Your home uses this power first. What happens to any extra depends on whether you have a battery:

- **With a Cell-10 battery:** the extra charges the battery. When the battery is full, surplus power is exported to the grid.
- **Without a battery:** surplus power is exported to the grid.

When the sun is not shining, your home draws from the battery (if fitted and charged) and then from the grid.

**Simple energy flow:**

`Sun → L-450 panels → hybrid inverter → home → battery → grid`

The monitoring app shows this flow in real time.

### [S2.2] L-450 solar panels

Each L-450 panel is rated at **450 W** under standard test conditions.

Key points:

- **Number of panels:** depends on your quote. The inverter is matched to the panel count (see [S2.3]).
- **Output per panel:** with typical conditions in Riverton, one panel produces about **591 kWh per year**. This estimate is based on 450 W × 4.5 peak sun hours × 365 days × 0.8 system efficiency, rounded to 591 kWh.
- **Performance warranty:** 25 years. Panels slowly lose output as they age, which is normal. The warranty covers performance falling below the guaranteed level.
- **Maintenance:** panels have no moving parts and need no servicing. Keep them free of shade and debris.

> **Note:** The 591 kWh figure is an average estimate. Your actual output will vary by season, weather, roof direction, and shading.

### [S2.3] HX5 and HX8 hybrid inverters

The inverter is the brain of your system. It converts DC to AC, manages the battery, talks to the monitoring app, and shuts the system down safely if it detects a fault.

| Model | Rated power | Suitable for |
|---|---|---|
| **HX5** | 5 kW | Systems of up to 11 panels |
| **HX8** | 8 kW | Systems of 12 to 18 panels |

Systems with more than 18 panels need a free site survey, and Lumora assigns the inverter after the survey.

Both models:

- Are "hybrid", meaning they work with or without a battery, and can add a Cell-10 later
- Have a display and status LEDs (see Section 3)
- Connect to your home Wi-Fi for monitoring
- Carry a **10-year warranty**

Inverters make a faint hum or fan noise when working hard on a sunny afternoon. This is normal. A casing that is **hot to the touch**, however, is an urgent situation (see [S1.4]).

### [S2.4] Cell-10 battery

The Cell-10 stores **10 kWh** of energy. It charges from surplus solar power and discharges when your home needs more than the panels are producing, such as in the evening.

Key points:

- **Warranty:** 10 years or 6,000 cycles, whichever comes first. One cycle is one full charge and discharge.
- **Capacity in practice:** a battery cannot be used down to absolute zero, so the app shows a "usable" percentage rather than raw kWh.
- **Placement:** the battery must stay in its installed location with clear space around it (see [S1.5]).
- **Adding later:** if you did not buy a battery at installation, Lumora can add a Cell-10 to a compatible HX5 or HX8 inverter at a later date.

A rough guide to its use: a 10 kWh battery can run a typical set of essential household loads (fridge, lights, internet, a few small devices) for many hours. Heavy loads like electric ovens and heaters drain it much faster.

### [S2.5] Monitoring app

The Lumora monitoring app is **free** and works on phones and tablets.

What you can see:

- Live solar production in watts
- Today's, this month's, and this year's energy produced in kWh
- Home consumption and grid import/export
- Battery charge level and status (if fitted)
- Alerts and fault notices
- An estimate of money saved

**Savings estimate:** the app values your solar energy at **$0.18 per kWh**, the grid price Lumora uses for savings estimates. For example, a single L-450 panel producing 591 kWh a year corresponds to about $106 in annual savings (591 × $0.18 = $106.38).

To set up the app:

1. Download the Lumora app from your phone's app store.
2. Create an account using the email address you gave Lumora at installation.
3. Enter the inverter serial number from the label on the inverter's side.
4. Confirm the inverter is connected to your home Wi-Fi.

If you cannot connect, call Support. Staff will only discuss your own account and system, and will never share another customer's information.

### [S2.6] Mounting and wiring

Your panels are fixed to the roof with Lumora's mounting kit, and the wiring kit carries power from the roof to the inverter and switchboard. Both are installed by Lumora technicians. The installation is covered by the **5-year workmanship warranty**. If you notice loose cables, a rattling panel, or a roof leak near the mounts, contact Support.

### [S2.7] Getting help and support hours

Lumora Support is available **Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 18:00**. For anything urgent (burning smell, smoke, sparks, hot inverter casing), follow [S1.4] first, then call. For non-urgent questions, you can contact support during opening hours, and for system faults you can also check the app.

## Section 3: Inverter Display and LED Meanings

### [S3.1] Overview of the display panel

The front of the inverter has a small screen and a row of LED lights. Together they tell you whether the system is working normally, starting up, waiting, or faulted.

The three LEDs from left to right:

1. **Power / Status LED**: overall system state
2. **Grid LED**: connection to the electricity grid
3. **Battery LED**: battery state (stays off if no battery is installed)

A fourth indicator, the **Wi-Fi icon** on the screen, shows whether the inverter is connected to the monitoring app.

### [S3.2] Power / Status LED

| LED colour and pattern | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Solid green** | Normal operation; producing power | Nothing |
| **Slow flashing green** (about once per second) | Starting up or waiting for enough sunlight (dawn, dusk, heavy cloud) | Nothing; it is normal |
| **Solid amber** | Warning; system still running but something needs attention | Check the app for the message; contact Support if it persists for more than 24 hours |
| **Flashing amber** | Reduced output, for example high temperature or a grid voltage problem | Check that vents are clear; if it continues, call Support |
| **Solid red** | Fault; inverter has stopped | Note any message on the screen, then call Support |
| **Flashing red** | Serious fault | Treat as a possible urgent situation; check [S1.4] and call Support |
| **Off** | No power to the inverter | At night this is normal. In daylight, check the isolators and your switchboard |

> **Warning:** If the casing is hot, or you smell burning, or see smoke or sparks, ignore the LED colour. Follow the urgent steps in [S1.4].

### [S3.3] Grid LED

| LED | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Solid green** | Connected to the grid and exporting or importing normally | Nothing |
| **Flashing green** | Grid detected; inverter is checking the supply before connecting | Wait up to a few minutes |
| **Solid amber** | Grid voltage or frequency outside the allowed range | Wait; the inverter will reconnect when stable |
| **Off** | No grid detected | A power outage is likely. Check whether neighbours have power |

During a grid outage, a system **without** a battery shuts down by design (see [S1.7]). This is not a fault.

### [S3.4] Battery LED

| LED | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Solid green** | Battery is healthy and holding charge |
| **Slow pulsing green** | Battery is charging |
| **Slow pulsing blue** | Battery is discharging, supplying your home |
| **Solid amber** | Battery is low (about 10% or less usable charge) or has a warning |
| **Solid red** | Battery fault. Call Support |
| **Off** | No battery fitted, or battery in standby |

A battery that is hot to touch, smells of burning, or is swollen is an **urgent** situation. Follow [S1.4].

### [S3.5] The screen

The screen cycles through several views. Press the button below the screen to move between them.

1. **Now:** current output in kW
2. **Today:** energy produced today in kWh
3. **Total:** lifetime energy produced in kWh
4. **Battery:** charge percentage (if fitted)
5. **Network:** Wi-Fi status and signal strength
6. **Messages:** any warnings or fault codes

Fault codes are in the format **E-xx** (for example, E-07). When you call Support, read out the code and the inverter serial number from the side label. This helps the team identify the problem faster.

### [S3.6] Wi-Fi icon

| Icon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Solid | Connected to Wi-Fi and the Lumora cloud |
| Flashing | Searching for or connecting to Wi-Fi |
| Crossed out | Not connected |

If the Wi-Fi icon is crossed out, the system still produces power normally. Only the app data is affected. Common causes are a router restart, a changed Wi-Fi password, or a weak signal.

**To reconnect:**

1. Check that your home internet works on another device.
2. Restart your router and wait 5 minutes.
3. Look at the inverter's Network screen. If it is still crossed out, open the app and follow the Wi-Fi setup steps.
4. If that does not work, contact Support during opening hours (Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 18:00).

### [S3.7] Restarting the inverter safely

A restart can clear a minor warning. Do this only if there is no urgent sign (heat, smell, smoke, sparks).

1. Switch off the **AC isolator**.
2. Switch off the **DC isolator**.
3. Wait **5 minutes**.
4. Switch on the **DC isolator**.
5. Switch on the **AC isolator**.
6. Wait up to 5 minutes for the LED to turn green.

If the same fault returns after a restart, do not repeat it more than once. Contact Support.

## Section 4: Daily Operation and Seasonal Output

### [S4.1] Normal day, hour by hour

You do not need to operate the system. It runs automatically. A typical sunny day looks like this:

| Time (approx.) | What happens |
|---|---|
| Before sunrise | Inverter is idle or in standby; LED off or slow flashing green |
| Early morning | Panels start producing; LED flashes green, then turns solid green |
| Mid-morning | Output climbs; your home uses solar first |
| Midday to early afternoon | Peak production; surplus charges the battery or exports to the grid |
| Late afternoon | Output falls; battery (if fitted) takes over as solar fades |
| Evening | Home runs from battery, then from the grid |
| Night | Panels produce nothing; inverter idles |

Production never reaches zero in a clean way. On cloudy days, output may be only a fraction of a clear day, and it can rise and fall quickly as clouds pass.

### [S4.2] Getting the most from your system

You can improve how much of your solar energy you use directly in your home:

- **Run big appliances during the day.** Washing machines, dishwashers, and dryers use solar power when they run at midday.
- **Use timers.** Delay-start features let you run appliances when the sun is strongest.
- **Avoid running many heavy loads at once** in the evening if you have a battery, because they drain it quickly.
- **Check the app weekly.** A sudden drop in daily production on a clear day may mean shade, a dirty panel, or a fault.

Every kWh you produce and use at home displaces a kWh you would otherwise buy from the grid. Lumora's savings estimates use $0.18 per kWh.

### [S4.3] What output to expect

Lumora's quotes use a standard estimate for each L-450 panel:

**450 W × 4.5 peak sun hours × 365 days × 0.8 system efficiency = 591.3 kWh per year, or about 591 kWh.**

To estimate your system's annual output, multiply 591 kWh by your panel count. Estimated annual savings are that output multiplied by $0.18 per kWh.

| Panels | Estimated annual output | Estimated annual savings |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | 3,546 kWh | $638.28 |
| 10 | 5,910 kWh | $1,063.80 |
| 14 | 8,274 kWh | $1,489.32 |
| 18 | 10,638 kWh | $1,914.84 |

*(Figures use 591 kWh per panel and assume all production offsets grid use.)*

Your real figures will differ. This is why Lumora gives estimates, not guarantees.

### [S4.4] Seasonal changes

Solar output changes through the year. In Riverton you should expect:

- **Summer:** the longest days and strongest sun. Highest daily output. The inverter may run warm on hot afternoons and reduce output slightly to protect itself. This is normal if the casing is warm but not hot.
- **Autumn and spring:** moderate output. Good for steady production.
- **Winter:** the shortest days, lowest sun angle, and the most cloud. Daily output may be well below the yearly average. Your battery may not fully charge on some days.

The 591 kWh per panel figure is a **yearly average**. A summer month will usually produce more than one-twelfth of the annual figure, and a winter month will produce less.

### [S4.5] Weather and shading

| Condition | Effect on output |
|---|---|
| Clear sky | Best output |
| Thin cloud | Moderate reduction |
| Heavy cloud or rain | Large reduction |
| Snow or ice on panels | Output may be near zero until it melts or slides off. Do not climb up to clear it |
| Dust, leaves, bird droppings | Small to moderate reduction |
| Shade from a tree, chimney, or building | Can reduce output of affected panels, and in some layouts the whole string |

If a new tree or structure begins to shade your panels, tell Lumora. A technician can advise whether it will make a meaningful difference.

### [S4.6] Reading your app

Use these checks to see whether your system is performing normally:

1. **Compare similar days.** Compare two clear days in the same month. They should produce similar totals.
2. **Look at the daily curve.** A healthy graph rises through the morning to a peak around midday and falls through the afternoon. Large dips on a clear day may indicate shade or a fault.
3. **Check the battery.** On a sunny day, a battery in normal use should charge in the day and discharge in the evening.
4. **Watch for alerts.** The app sends notices for warnings and faults. Do not ignore a repeated alert.

### [S4.7] When low output is not a fault

The following are normal and need no action:

- Low output at dawn, dusk, and on heavy overcast days
- Lower totals in winter than in summer
- Zero output at night
- The inverter limiting output on very hot afternoons, if the casing is warm but not hot
- The system shutting down during a grid outage when no battery is fitted

### [S4.8] When to call Support

Contact Lumora Support (Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 18:00) if:

- Output is much lower than usual on clear days for more than a few days
- The Status LED is solid amber for more than 24 hours or is red
- The app shows a repeated alert or fault code
- The Wi-Fi icon stays crossed out after you tried the steps in [S3.6]
- The battery does not charge or discharge as expected
- You notice damaged cables, loose panels, or a roof leak near the mounts

Have your inverter serial number and any fault code ready. Staff will verify you are the account holder before discussing your system.

For **burning smell, smoke, sparks, or a hot inverter casing**, do not wait for a convenient time. Switch off the AC and DC isolators, keep away, and call right away. A technician will be dispatched the same day.

## Section 5: Error Codes E01-E24

### [S5.1] How to read an error code

When the system detects a problem, the HX inverter display shows a code in the form **E##** and the status light changes color. The same code appears in the Lumora monitoring app under **Alerts**.

- **Amber light, steady:** a warning. The system is still running or will restart on its own.
- **Amber light, flashing:** the system has paused. Follow the steps for your code.
- **Red light:** the system has shut down for protection. Do not restart it more than once (see [S5.3]).

Codes are listed below in order. Codes marked **URGENT** follow the safety procedure in [S5.2].

### [S5.2] URGENT safety procedure

Any **burning smell, smoke, sparks, or a hot inverter casing** is URGENT, whatever code is showing. Also treat **E09** (when accompanied by any of these signs) and **E21** as urgent.

1. Do not touch the inverter or battery.
2. Switch off the **AC isolator** (the switch on or near your switchboard).
3. Switch off the **DC isolator** (the switch on or beside the inverter).
4. Keep people and pets away from the equipment.
5. Call Lumora Support. A technician is dispatched the **same day**.

**Warning:** Never open the inverter or battery cover. Never pour water on equipment. If there is visible fire, leave the building and call emergency services first.

Support hours are Mon-Sat, 8:00-18:00. If you are outside these hours, do steps 1-4 and call as soon as support opens, or call emergency services if anyone is in danger.

### [S5.3] Before you call: the one-restart rule

For non-urgent codes, you may try one restart:

1. Switch off the AC isolator, then the DC isolator.
2. Wait **5 minutes**.
3. Switch on the DC isolator, then the AC isolator.
4. Wait up to 5 minutes for the display to show normal status.

If the code returns, do not keep restarting. Repeated restarts can hide a fault and may shorten equipment life. Note the code, the time, and what the display shows, then call.

### [S5.4] Error code list

**E01 – GRID VOLTAGE LOW**
- *Meaning:* The grid voltage at your home has dropped below the allowed range.
- *Customer can do:* Wait 15 minutes; the inverter restarts itself when voltage returns. Check whether neighbours have power problems.
- *Call Lumora if:* The code appears more than 3 times in a day or lasts over 1 hour after power is normal.

**E02 – GRID FREQUENCY OUT OF RANGE**
- *Meaning:* The grid frequency is unstable.
- *Customer can do:* Wait 15 minutes. No action needed.
- *Call Lumora if:* It repeats daily for 3 days.

**E03 – GRID LOSS**
- *Meaning:* The grid is down (outage). Solar output stops for safety unless backup mode is active (see [S6.5]).
- *Customer can do:* Check your household breakers. Contact your grid provider for outage information.
- *Call Lumora if:* The grid is back and the system does not restart after one restart.

**E04 – AC BREAKER TRIPPED**
- *Meaning:* The breaker feeding the inverter has opened.
- *Customer can do:* Reset the breaker once. If it trips again straight away, leave it off.
- *Call Lumora if:* It trips a second time.

**E05 – INVERTER TEMP HIGH**
- *Meaning:* The inverter is too warm and is reducing output.
- *Customer can do:* Make sure vents are clear of dust, boxes, and covers. Allow it to cool for 30 minutes.
- *Call Lumora if:* It returns on cool days. If the casing is hot to the touch, treat it as URGENT ([S5.2]).

**E06 – FAN FAULT**
- *Meaning:* The cooling fan is not spinning as expected.
- *Customer can do:* Check that nothing blocks the fan grille. Do one restart.
- *Call Lumora if:* The code remains. Output may be limited until repaired.

**E07 – PV STRING 1 LOW VOLTAGE**
- *Meaning:* Panel string 1 is producing less voltage than expected (heavy shade, dirt, snow, or a wiring issue).
- *Customer can do:* Check for new shade or debris on panels. Do not climb on the roof.
- *Call Lumora if:* It persists on a clear day at midday.

**E08 – PV STRING 2 LOW VOLTAGE**
- Same as E07, for string 2. Systems with a single string will not show this code.

**E09 – BATTERY TEMP HIGH**
- *Meaning:* The Cell-10 battery is above its safe temperature. Charging and discharging are reduced or stopped.
- *Customer can do:* Make sure the battery has airflow and is out of direct sun. Do not stack items against it. Wait 1 hour for it to cool.
- *Call Lumora if:* The code returns within 24 hours. **URGENT** if you notice a burning smell, smoke, swelling, or a hot casing: follow [S5.2].

**E10 – BATTERY TEMP LOW**
- *Meaning:* The battery is too cold to charge safely.
- *Customer can do:* Wait; it resumes when warmer. No action needed.
- *Call Lumora if:* It lasts more than 3 days.

**E11 – BATTERY COMMUNICATION LOST**
- *Meaning:* The inverter cannot talk to the battery.
- *Customer can do:* Do one restart.
- *Call Lumora if:* The code remains after restart.

**E12 – BATTERY LOW**
- *Meaning:* Battery charge has reached its minimum reserve.
- *Customer can do:* Reduce usage; the battery recharges from solar.
- *Call Lumora if:* It stays low for several sunny days.

**E13 – BATTERY OVER-CURRENT**
- *Meaning:* Too much power was drawn from or sent to the battery.
- *Customer can do:* Turn off large loads (oven, heater, electric vehicle charger) and restart once.
- *Call Lumora if:* It repeats.

**E14 – INVERTER COMMUNICATION ERROR**
- *Meaning:* Internal communication problem between inverter modules.
- *Customer can do:* Do one restart.
- *Call Lumora if:* It remains.

**E15 – METER COMMUNICATION LOST**
- *Meaning:* The energy meter is not responding, so export and consumption data may be wrong.
- *Customer can do:* Do one restart.
- *Call Lumora if:* It persists beyond 24 hours.

**E16 – WI-FI MODULE FAULT**
- *Meaning:* The inverter's Wi-Fi module has failed to start. Power production is not affected.
- *Customer can do:* Do one restart, then see Section 7.
- *Call Lumora if:* The app still shows offline after 24 hours.

**E17 – GRID OVERVOLTAGE**
- *Meaning:* Grid voltage at your connection is above the allowed range, often when many neighbouring systems export at midday. The inverter pauses output to protect your home and the grid.
- *Customer can do:* Wait 15-30 minutes; it normally restarts automatically. Do not adjust any inverter settings. Note times when it occurs.
- *Call Lumora if:* It happens more than 3 times in a day or on several days in a row. We may need to adjust voltage settings, which only a technician can do.

**E18 – DC INPUT REVERSED**
- *Meaning:* Panel polarity is incorrect.
- *Customer can do:* Switch off both isolators and do not restart.
- *Call Lumora:* Immediately.

**E19 – SURGE PROTECTION TRIGGERED**
- *Meaning:* The surge protector has absorbed a voltage spike (for example, after lightning).
- *Customer can do:* Do one restart.
- *Call Lumora if:* The code remains or the surge protector indicator is red.

**E20 – RELAY FAULT**
- *Meaning:* An internal switching relay is not operating properly.
- *Customer can do:* Do one restart.
- *Call Lumora:* If it returns; the inverter will need service.

**E21 – ISOLATION FAULT (URGENT)**
- *Meaning:* The system has detected current leaking to earth. This can be caused by damaged cable insulation or water entering a connector, and may present an electric shock or fire risk.
- *Customer can do:* Follow [S5.2] immediately: switch off the AC isolator and DC isolator, and keep away. Do not restart.
- *Call Lumora:* Immediately. A technician is dispatched the same day.

**E22 – DC CURRENT INJECTION**
- *Meaning:* The inverter has detected abnormal DC current on the AC side.
- *Customer can do:* Do one restart.
- *Call Lumora if:* It returns.

**E23 – FIRMWARE UPDATE FAILED**
- *Meaning:* An update did not complete.

## Section 6: Battery Care and Backup Mode

### [S6.1] About your Cell-10 battery

The Cell-10 stores **10 kWh** of energy. It charges from surplus solar energy during the day and supplies your home in the evening, reducing what you buy from the grid at $0.18 per kWh (the price used in Lumora savings estimates).

The battery is covered for **10 years or 6,000 cycles**, whichever comes first. One cycle is one full charge and discharge, or the equivalent across partial uses.

### [S6.2] Where the battery should be kept

- Keep the area around it clear: at least **30 cm** at the sides and top for airflow.
- Keep it out of direct sunlight and away from heaters.
- Do not store paint, fuel, gas bottles, or cardboard near it.
- Keep it dry. Do not use a hose or pressure washer near it.
- Do not move, tilt, or unmount it. Contact Lumora if relocation is needed.

### [S6.3] Everyday care

1. **Check the display or app monthly.** Look for amber or red alerts.
2. **Dust the surface** with a dry cloth every 3 months, with the system switched off at both isolators if you need to reach near vents.
3. **Do not drain it to zero on purpose.** Leave the reserve setting at the default unless advised otherwise.
4. **Keep firmware updated.** Updates install overnight when Wi-Fi is connected.
5. **Do not open covers or touch cables.** There are no user-serviceable parts.

### [S6.4] Understanding battery settings

In the app under **Battery**, you can choose:

| Mode | What it does | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-use** (default) | Charges from solar, supplies the home when solar is low | Most households |
| **Backup priority** | Holds a larger reserve for outages | Areas with frequent power cuts |
| **Charge only** | Charges but does not discharge | Short-term use when advised by support |

**Reserve level:** The default reserve is 20%. Raising it to 50% means more power during an outage but less daily savings. Changes apply within a few minutes.

### [S6.5] Backup mode during a power outage

If your system was installed with backup capability, the battery supplies power to your **essential loads circuit** when the grid fails.

**What happens:**
1. The grid drops (E03 may show).
2. Within a few seconds the inverter switches to backup.
3. Lights and circuits on the backup circuit stay on.
4. When the grid returns, the system reconnects automatically after a short safety check.

**Important:**
- Only circuits connected to the essential loads panel are powered. Ask your installer which circuits those are.
- Large appliances (ovens, central heating, welders, some air conditioners) may overload backup and are best left off.
- The HX5 supports up to 5 kW of backup load and the HX8 up to 8 kW, in each case less if battery charge is low.
- Solar panels can recharge the battery during the day, even during an outage.
- Estimate runtime: usable energy divided by your load. For example, a 500 W load with 8 kWh available runs roughly 16 hours.

### [S6.6] Preparing for a storm or planned outage

1. Switch to **Backup priority** at least 24 hours before.
2. Confirm charge is above 90% in the app.
3. Switch off non-essential appliances.
4. Do not switch off isolators unless instructed or if there is a safety concern.

### [S6.7] Long absence

For holidays of up to 4 weeks, leave the system on in Self-use mode. If the battery is to be left unused for longer, contact Lumora for advice; do not switch it off yourself for extended periods.

### [S6.8] Signs of battery trouble

Call Lumora if you see:
- Repeated E09, E10, E11, E12, or E13
- Runtime noticeably falling over several months
- The app showing capacity far below 10 kWh when fully charged
- Casing that looks swollen or deformed

**URGENT:** burning smell, smoke, sparks, or a hot casing. Follow [S5.2].

### [S7.4] Troubleshooting: app shows "Offline"

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Wi-Fi light off | Inverter off or module fault | Check the display is on; see E16 |
| Wi-Fi light blinking slowly | Cannot find router | Move router closer or reboot it |
| Wi-Fi light blinking fast | Wrong password | Repeat setup in [S7.3] |
| Light steady, app offline | Router has no internet | Test internet on another device |
| Worked before, now offline | Router or password changed | Repeat setup |

### [S7.5] Step-by-step fixes

1. **Reboot your router.** Unplug for 30 seconds and plug in. Wait 5 minutes.
2. **Check the network band.** Make sure 2.4 GHz is enabled. Some routers combine both bands under one name; if setup fails, temporarily split them or ask your internet provider.
3. **Avoid special characters.** Some passwords with unusual symbols cause trouble. Try a simple temporary password for testing.
4. **Reduce distance.** A Wi-Fi extender placed halfway between router and inverter often fixes weak signal.
5. **Check router security.** Use WPA2. Very old (WEP) or hidden networks may fail.
6. **Check device limits.** Some routers limit the number of connected devices.
7. **Restart the inverter** using the one-restart rule in [S5.3].

## Section 8: Cleaning and Maintenance

### [S8.1] Why maintenance matters

A Lumora system is designed to run for decades with very little attention. Even so, a small amount of regular care keeps output close to the figures in your quote. Our savings estimates assume a system efficiency of 0.8, which already allows for normal dust, heat and wiring losses. Neglected panels, blocked vents or ignored alerts can push your real performance below that figure.

This section explains what you can safely do yourself, what you should leave to a technician, and how to recognise a problem early.

### [S8.2] Safety rules before you touch anything

Read these rules before any cleaning or inspection.

- **Never climb onto the roof** to clean panels. Use ground-level methods only, or book a technician.
- **Never open the inverter casing, battery enclosure or any wiring box.** There are live DC and AC circuits inside, and opening them may void your workmanship warranty.
- **Panels produce electricity whenever there is daylight.** Switching off the isolators does not make the panels themselves "dead."
- **Do not use a pressure washer.** It can force water into connectors and damage the panel seals.
- **Do not clean in strong midday sun.** Cold water on hot glass can stress it, and cleaning liquid dries into streaks.
- **Do not work near the system during a storm** or when surfaces are wet and slippery.

> **URGENT:** If you notice a burning smell, smoke, sparks or a hot inverter casing, stop what you are doing. Switch off the **AC isolator** and the **DC isolator**, keep away from the equipment, and call Lumora Support straight away. A technician is dispatched the same day. See [S8.9].

### [S8.3] Maintenance schedule at a glance

| Task | How often | Who does it |
|---|---|---|
| Check the monitoring app for output and alerts | Weekly | You |
| Visual check of panels from the ground | Monthly | You |
| Clean panels (ground-level method) | Every 3-6 months, or as needed | You |
| Check inverter and battery vents are clear | Monthly | You |
| Wipe inverter and battery exterior | Every 3 months | You |
| Check cables and isolators for visible damage | Every 6 months | You (look only) |
| Professional inspection | Every 2 years (recommended) | Lumora technician |

### [S8.4] Cleaning your panels

**What you need**
- A soft sponge, soft brush or a squeegee on an extension pole
- A garden hose with a gentle spray setting
- Clean lukewarm or cool water
- Optional: a small amount of mild, non-abrasive dish soap

**Steps**
1. Choose early morning, late afternoon or an overcast day, when the panels are cool.
2. Rinse the panels with a gentle spray to loosen dust, pollen and leaves.
3. If needed, mix a few drops of mild dish soap in a bucket of water. Wipe the glass lightly with the soft sponge or brush.
4. Rinse thoroughly with clean water so no soap film remains.
5. Let the panels air-dry, or draw a clean squeegee across the glass from top to bottom.
6. Check the monitoring app later in the day to confirm output looks normal.

**Do not use**
- Abrasive pads, steel wool or scrapers
- Strong solvents, bleach, ammonia or glass cleaners with harsh chemicals
- Hot water
- Pressure washers

### [S8.5] How often to clean

In Riverton, most customers find that rain keeps panels reasonably clean. Clean more often if:

- Your panels are close to trees (leaves, sap, bird droppings).
- Construction or roadworks nearby create heavy dust.
- Your roof has a shallow tilt, so rain does not wash the glass well.
- The monitoring app shows output lower than usual on clear days.

Bird droppings and sap should be removed as soon as you notice them. Left in place, they can shade individual cells and reduce output more than an even layer of dust would.

### [S8.6] Using the monitoring app to spot problems

The Lumora monitoring app is free and shows daily and monthly production. Check it weekly.

**What normal looks like**
- A smooth curve rising in the morning, peaking near midday, and falling in the evening.
- Lower output on cloudy days, and near zero at night.
- Output that is broadly similar to the same season in earlier weeks.

**Signs to investigate**
- Production is much lower than neighbouring days with the same weather.
- The curve has sudden dips or flat tops on clear days.
- The app shows an inverter fault, communication loss or battery warning.
- No production at all during daylight.

**First steps if output looks low**
1. Look at the panels from the ground for shade, debris or dirt.
2. Clean the panels using [S8.4] if they look dirty.
3. Check that the inverter display is lit and shows no fault code.
4. Wait one full sunny day and compare the app again.
5. If output is still low, contact Support and have your fault code or app screenshot ready.

### [S8.7] Inverter care (HX5 and HX8)

- Keep a clear space around the inverter. Do not store boxes, cloths or other items against it.
- Make sure the vents are free of dust, cobwebs and insects. Wipe the outside with a dry or slightly damp cloth. Never spray water on it.
- The casing may feel warm during normal operation, especially on hot afternoons. A casing that is **hot** to the touch is not normal and is urgent. Follow the safety steps in [S8.9].
- Do not paint over labels or cover the display.
- Note any fault codes before calling Support. Do not repeatedly switch the isolators on and off to "reset" a fault.

### [S8.8] Battery care (Cell-10)

- Keep the area around the battery dry, ventilated and free of clutter.
- Do not stack items on or against the battery.
- Avoid placing heat sources or flammable materials nearby.
- Wipe the exterior with a dry or lightly damp cloth every 3 months.
- Leave the battery connected so that it can manage its own charging. Long periods of being switched off can affect its condition.
- Watch the app for battery warnings, such as a failure to charge or discharge.

The Cell-10 battery is covered for 10 years or 6,000 cycles, whichever comes first. See Section 9.

### [S8.9] Urgent situations

Treat any of the following as urgent:

- A burning smell near the panels, inverter, battery or wiring
- Smoke
- Sparks
- A hot inverter casing

**What to do**
1. Do not touch the equipment.
2. If it is safe to reach them, switch off the **AC isolator** and the **DC isolator**.
3. Keep yourself, your family and pets away from the equipment.
4. Call Lumora Support and report the problem as urgent. A technician is dispatched the same day.
5. If there is fire or any danger to life, contact emergency services first.

Do not attempt to inspect, repair or reconnect anything yourself.

### [S8.10] Professional inspections

We recommend a professional inspection every 2 years. A technician checks mounting, connectors, cable condition, isolators, inverter and battery status, and system output. Keep any inspection records, as they can help if you later make a warranty claim.

Servicing carried out by someone other than a Lumora technician or an approved professional may affect your workmanship warranty.

### [S8.11] Weather and seasonal notes

- **After storms:** Check from the ground for loose panels, displaced cables or debris. If anything looks damaged, contact Support and do not approach the equipment.
- **Dust season:** Clean more often when dust is heavy.
- **Leaf fall:** Clear debris from around the array and gutters, where it is safe to do so from the ground.
